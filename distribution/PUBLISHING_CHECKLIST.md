# Publishing Checklist

## Status

Draft only. Nothing in `distribution/` publishes automatically.

## Manual Login Required

- GitHub account for Pages or release notes updates
- Hugging Face account for Space and Dataset publication
- MCP registry or directory account for listing submission
- LinkedIn account for social post publication
- Hacker News account for post submission
- Dev.to account for article publication
- fabrication or design community account for forum posting

## Review Before Publishing

- confirm all examples remain local-only and sandbox-only
- confirm no copy implies real manufacturing approval
- confirm no copy implies vendor selection
- confirm no copy implies production readiness
- confirm all guardrail language is preserved
- confirm screenshots or examples do not leak private data

## Required Manual Steps

1. Review each draft with a human approver.
2. Log in manually to the destination platform.
3. Paste or upload the reviewed artifact.
4. Re-check links and formatting.
5. Publish manually.

## Forbidden Automation

- no auto-post script
- no token-based background publish flow
- no webhook-triggered publication
- no browser automation for publication
