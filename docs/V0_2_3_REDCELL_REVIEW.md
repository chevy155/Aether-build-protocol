# v0.2.3 Red Cell Review

## Role 1: Real Fabricator

- Strongest objection: This is not a fabricator-ready package because the CAD and print artifacts are only metadata placeholders and the packet omits the real drawing depth needed for quoting with confidence.
- Evidence supporting the objection: `simulations/industrial_hook/builder_workspace/cad/utility_hook_v1.mock_sldprt.json` says "Mock CAD metadata only."; `simulations/industrial_hook/builder_workspace/cad/utility_hook_print_rev0.mock_pdf.json` says "mock_pdf_print_metadata"; `simulations/industrial_hook/fabricator_workspace/feasibility_review.json` preserves missing substrate, missing fastener specification, missing engineering certification, and missing load rating.
- Risk severity: HIGH
- Whether it blocks release: No, not for a local-only simulation release.
- Recommended fix or deferral: Keep as-is for v0.2.3, but add bad-input and rejection scenarios plus real reviewer feedback in alpha feedback.

## Role 2: Mechanical Engineer

- Strongest objection: The simulation proves data handoff, not engineering adequacy; there is no geometry analysis, stress review, fastener design, or substrate analysis.
- Evidence supporting the objection: `simulations/industrial_hook/builder_workspace/intake/builder_project_intake.yaml` states engineering certification not provided; `simulations/industrial_hook/aether_workspace/build_packet.yaml` preserves "Actual load requirement unknown"; `simulations/industrial_hook/monitor_workspace/outcome_event.json` is explicitly seed-only.
- Risk severity: HIGH
- Whether it blocks release: No, if the release is clearly positioned as a local-only simulation harness.
- Recommended fix or deferral: Defer to future real-world integration; do not let reviewer messaging imply engineering validation.

## Role 3: Safety/Liability Reviewer

- Strongest objection: A hook-shaped part plus a complete quote-and-outcome chain can be misread as a stronger safety claim than the code actually supports.
- Evidence supporting the objection: `simulations/industrial_hook/outputs/simulation_report.md` ends in PASS; `simulations/industrial_hook/aether_workspace/quote_readiness_score.json` gives 100/100; the part is a hook artifact with missing load requirement and no certification.
- Risk severity: HIGH
- Whether it blocks release: No, but it is a messaging risk.
- Recommended fix or deferral: Keep the current disclaimers, and explicitly brief reviewers that PASS means protocol-pass, not safety-pass.

## Role 4: Protocol Architect

- Strongest objection: The harness demonstrates artifact generation but not provenance integrity, adversarial robustness, or multi-party interoperability.
- Evidence supporting the objection: `scripts/simulate_industrial_hook_pipeline.py` builds every artifact inside one deterministic script; `simulations/industrial_hook/monitor_workspace/simulation_trace.json` has lineage but no hashing; `simulations/industrial_hook/monitor_workspace/guardrail_audit.json` is phrase-scan based.
- Risk severity: MEDIUM
- Whether it blocks release: No.
- Recommended fix or deferral: Add artifact hashing/provenance and negative-path scenarios in v0.2.4 or v0.3.

## Role 5: Open-Source Reviewer

- Strongest objection: The demo is understandable, but some outputs may look more authoritative than the underlying evidence supports.
- Evidence supporting the objection: `simulations/industrial_hook/aether_workspace/quote_readiness_score.md` shows 100/100 with no gaps; `simulations/industrial_hook/outputs/simulation_report.md` shows PASS; the fabricator response is from a named simulator, not an external source.
- Risk severity: MEDIUM
- Whether it blocks release: No.
- Recommended fix or deferral: Document the difference between schema/readiness success and fabrication adequacy in reviewer materials.

## Role 6: Future Customer/Operator

- Strongest objection: I still do not know whether a real shop would trust this packet or whether the quote is economically meaningful.
- Evidence supporting the objection: `simulations/industrial_hook/fabricator_workspace/quote_response.json` is generated by the deterministic simulator; `simulations/industrial_hook/fabricator_workspace/feasibility_review.json` has no connection to external shop policy; `docs/END_TO_END_SIMULATION_SPEC.md` calls out no real fabricator quote.
- Risk severity: MEDIUM
- Whether it blocks release: No.
- Recommended fix or deferral: Gather real reviewer reactions first, then decide whether to pursue real fabricator pilot inputs.

## Role 7: Skeptical Investor/Strategist

- Strongest objection: The harness is a good demo, but it does not yet prove network effects, external adoption, or real commercial pull.
- Evidence supporting the objection: only one simulated fabricator profile exists at `simulations/industrial_hook/fabricator_workspace/fabricator_profile.json`; no external interaction exists in the repo; no provenance or interoperability benchmark exists.
- Risk severity: MEDIUM
- Whether it blocks release: No.
- Recommended fix or deferral: Defer commercialization claims and use this release strictly as a reviewer-legibility milestone.

## Specific Red-Cell Issues

- Mock CAD is not real CAD.
  Evidence: `simulations/industrial_hook/builder_workspace/cad/utility_hook_v1.mock_sldprt.json` and `simulations/industrial_hook/aether_workspace/build_packet.yaml` both call out mock metadata only.
- Mock PDF is not a real print.
  Evidence: `simulations/industrial_hook/builder_workspace/cad/utility_hook_print_rev0.mock_pdf.json` is metadata only.
- No actual drawing geometry is parsed.
  Evidence: `scripts/simulate_industrial_hook_pipeline.py` builds packet fields from fixture values and metadata, not geometry parsing.
- The hook is not rated or certified for lifting use.
  Evidence: builder intake, drawing metadata, Build Packet safety notes, feasibility review, quote response exclusions, and simulation report all preserve that boundary.
- Fabricator quote is simulated.
  Evidence: `simulations/industrial_hook/fabricator_workspace/quote_response.json` responder type is `fabricator_simulator`.
- Feasibility logic is simplified.
  Evidence: `scripts/simulate_industrial_hook_pipeline.py` checks only declared capabilities, thickness, tolerance, finish presence, and material compatibility.
- Quote response is deterministic.
  Evidence: price, lead time, exclusions, and questions are hard-coded in `scripts/simulate_industrial_hook_pipeline.py`.
- Outcome event could be misread if skimmed.
  Evidence: `simulations/industrial_hook/monitor_workspace/outcome_event.json` includes cost and timeline fields even though it is clearly marked seed-only.
- Generated artifacts may look more authoritative than they are.
  Evidence: PASS statuses appear in `simulations/industrial_hook/aether_workspace/validation_report.md`, `simulations/industrial_hook/fabricator_workspace/quote_response_validation_report.md`, and `simulations/industrial_hook/outputs/simulation_report.md`.
- Reviewers may misread the simulation as fabrication readiness.
  Evidence: `simulations/industrial_hook/aether_workspace/quote_readiness_score.json` reports 100/100 while key real-world unknowns remain unresolved.
- Scoring may imply quality judgment beyond evidence.
  Evidence: `simulations/industrial_hook/aether_workspace/quote_readiness_score.md` and `simulations/industrial_hook/aether_workspace/quote_comparison_summary.md` produce strong-looking scores from synthetic inputs.
- No real human approval event is captured.
  Evidence: artifacts preserve `human_review_required`, but no standalone approval object exists.
- No versioned artifact hash or provenance manifest exists yet.
  Evidence: `simulations/industrial_hook/monitor_workspace/simulation_trace.json` lists lineage only.
- No multiple-fabricator simulation exists.
  Evidence: one profile exists at `simulations/industrial_hook/fabricator_workspace/fabricator_profile.json`.
- No failure-path simulation exists.
  Evidence: `tests/test_industrial_hook_simulation.py` covers only the passing path.
- No bad or missing CAD package scenario exists.
  Evidence: harness always writes a complete mock builder package in `scripts/simulate_industrial_hook_pipeline.py`.
- No fabricator rejection scenario exists.
  Evidence: `simulations/industrial_hook/fabricator_workspace/feasibility_review.json` always lands on `REVIEWABLE_WITH_HUMAN_GATES` for the default scenario.

## Red Cell Bottom Line

v0.2.3 is releaseable as a local-only simulation harness because it proves a concrete protocol handoff and preserves its non-approval guardrails.

v0.2.3 is not evidence of manufacturability, engineering sufficiency, or real quoting readiness. Any public demo should say that directly and repeatedly.
