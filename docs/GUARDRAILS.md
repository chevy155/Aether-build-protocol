# Guardrails

## Hard Boundaries

- No autonomous action.
- No supplier outreach.
- No construction approval.
- No permit approval.
- No engineering approval.
- No payment flow.

## Required Safety And Review Flags

- Licensed trade flags must remain visible.
- Human approval is required before any external action.
- Safety-critical warnings must remain attached to the packet and downstream objects.
- Code review and permit review requirements must remain visible.
- Unknowns must never be deleted to make a packet appear quote-ready.
